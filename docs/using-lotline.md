# Use Lotline

Lotline helps a manufacturer, warehouse or shop answer: **Which batch went where, what was actually received, and what needs to come back?** A shared record makes missing quantities and recall follow-up visible.

1. Open [Lotline](https://supplyledger.vercel.app/) and choose **Start a workspace**. Create your own account.
2. Open the workspace menu. Add your product and storage site, then invite a warehouse or shop with access to its site. Share the invitation link yourself.
3. In **Trace**, commission a batch with its quantity. Record any split, merge or repackaging.
4. In **Handoffs**, create and dispatch a shipment. The receiving partner signs in and records the quantity it counted.
5. For a faulty batch, use **Incidents** to preview its affected descendants, activate holds and collect partner responses. Record recovered stock or explained losses before closing.
6. In **Evidence**, download the history and incident packet when you need a reviewable record.

For example, sending 500 packs and receiving 480 leaves **20 visibly unresolved**. If that batch is faulty, Lotline shows the connected stock and partners to contact, then tracks the return quantities. This can reduce manual follow-up, shipment disputes and unnecessary recalls.

The public deployment is a **temporary demonstration**. Its free database expires on **11 November 2026**. Use synthetic stock; arrange durable hosting and database recovery before relying on it for business records. Free hosting may take time to wake after inactivity.
